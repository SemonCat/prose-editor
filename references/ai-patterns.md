# AI-pattern diagnostic

Patterns are clues, not forbidden tokens. Judge function, frequency, genre, and source support.

## Content

Watch for inflated significance, promotional haze, vague attribution, superficial interpretation, forced balance, overgeneralized sourcing, generic conclusions, faux-insight framing that announces a neglected truth, and interpretive metadiscourse that tells the reader what matters instead of proving it. Prefer a sourced fact, a named actor, or deletion. Mark missing evidence instead of inventing it.

## Structure

Watch for repeated “not X but Y” pivots, negative runways, forced groups of three, false ranges, rhetorical setup with an immediate answer, dramatic colon reveals that turn a label into a setup and payoff, stacked punchy fragments, punch-line endings on every paragraph, uniform rhythm, summary sandwiches, fake-profound kickers, excessive headings or bold-label lists, headings restated below themselves, announcements of the next point, unsupported objections or fake alternatives, and false agency that hides who acted.

Keep a real contrast, real three-part set, passive voice, named objection, plausible alternative, functional colon, or intentional fragment when it expresses the meaning better. A colon, fragment, emoji, bold label, or contrast is not a defect by itself; change it only when it performs empty drama or breaks venue fit. Delete a denial when no source or reader raised the claim. When an option exists only to be rejected, keep the chosen approach and its reason instead.

Inspect repeated clause skeletons separately from sentence length: recurring subject–action–explanatory-tail sequences may flatten different relationships. Change only a sequence with an identifiable reader cost; preserve procedural symmetry, parallel requirements, and deliberate rhetoric. Equal-length sentences alone require no repair.

## Language

Watch for throat-clearing, sycophancy, chatbot sign-offs, inflated or fake-strong substitutes for “is” or “did”, vague jargon, synonym cycling, stacked hedges, fashionable vocabulary without domain meaning, mechanical punctuation, and mannered prose that substitutes flourish or ornamental metaphor for a direct statement. Prefer literal wording when it says the same thing more precisely. Do not run global replacement. A precise or quoted word is not a defect.

Do not treat a single punctuation choice, em dash, punctuation density, paragraph count, average paragraph length, or mean sentence length as model-agnostic evidence. Published directions vary by model release, corpus, genre, and counting method. Change them only for readability, venue fit, or an attributable author habit.

## Voice recovery

After cleanup ask whether the prose lost an actual opinion or uncertainty, turned specifics into summaries, became uniformly terse, added unsupported first person or attitude, or could appear unchanged under an unrelated author's name. Plain prose is acceptable; fabricated personality is not.

## Artifacts

Remove unintended collaboration phrases, model limitations, search commentary, unresolved placeholders, broken Markdown, citation handles, tool IDs, AI-added tracking parameters, decorative formatting that does not fit the venue, and editor meta-commentary the reader did not request. For a rewrite, labels such as “Rewritten” or “What changed”, repeated recaps, and generic follow-up offers are artifacts unless the venue or user calls for them.
